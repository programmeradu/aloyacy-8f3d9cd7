## Full description

Aloya is the easiest way to use the bus in Cyprus.

Plan a trip across the island in plain English ("tomorrow morning to Larnaca airport"), see live arrivals at every stop in Nicosia, Limassol, Larnaca, Paphos, and the intercity network, and get notified the moment your bus is approaching or running late.

What's inside

• Live arrivals from the official Cyprus Bus network, double-checked against real rider GPS so ghost trips and cancellations show up.
• Cross-island intercity intelligence — Aloya knows when a Nicosia–Limassol coach is the right call versus the urban network.
• Smart trip alerts that ping you to leave, board, and alight on time, even with the screen off.
• Save your Motion bus card on this device with a quick camera scan — no manual typing.
• Works offline: schedules are cached so you can plan a trip without signal.
• Designed in Cyprus with Cypriot riders. Light, fast, no clutter.

Aloya is free to use. Aloya Pro unlocks advanced alerts and trip chaining.

- Email address (only if you sign in) — Account management

Data not linked to you:
- Diagnostics (crash logs) — App functionality

Data not collected:
