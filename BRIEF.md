# Aloya 50-layer smart mobility research (Samuel, Oct 31 2026 19:28 EET)
Vision: Aloya is NOT a bus app. It is a smart mobility platform for Cyprus that integrates many partners and is open for others to integrate it. Audience: every urban commuter, with or without a car.

What Samuel raised:
- Notifications are dumb. When he starts a trip at home, it keeps notifying as if he is already at the stop. Before it alerts, it must compare his location, his walking time to the stop and the bus's live position. Example: "You're 10 min away, the bus is 5 min away, hurry or you'll miss it."
- Micromobility (bikes and scooters) is not only for the first or last mile. Any mode can combine with any public transport anywhere in a trip, including the middle.
- Routing should find a better stop a few blocks away or across the road, with more frequent or faster buses. It should suggest walking, cycling, scooting or a ride to that stop, instead of waiting 20 to 40 minutes at the nearest one.
- List every improvement, with no gaps and nothing dumb. Include ideas he has never mentioned. Use datasets and combine APIs.

Deliverable: a deep book of any length, built from 50 distinct layers, each researched properly (not shallow), then compiled into one book. QA of the app follows.

Each layer's section covers:
1) What the best in the world do, with sources.
2) The reality in Cyprus and the data available.
3) Aloya today, with its gaps. Read the code in repo programmeradu/aloyacy-8f3d9cd7, branch redesign-roam, where relevant.
4) Concrete improvements, numbered and specific: how to build each one, the data and APIs it needs, effort (S/M/L) and impact.
5) Sources.

Rules: never invent facts or numbers; cite sources; British spelling; no em or en dashes.

## Addendum (Samuel, Oct 31 19:31 EET)
- No limits: every area of Cyprus's mobility sector counts.
- The app should be intelligent at every phase of a multi-stop trip: before boarding, on the first bus, approaching the transfer, during it and after it. It keeps re-optimising for faster or new buses, alternatives, and micromobility in the middle of the trip.
- Goal: more people on public transport and less reliance on private cars, a real problem in Cyprus.
- BONUS part F: would Aloya's vision reduce car dependence, what else is needed, and how Aloya can contribute.
